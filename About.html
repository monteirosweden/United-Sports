📜 Our History

Founded in 2010, Sports United started as a small group of football, cricket, and basketball fans at the college. Today, it's the official club for all three sports.

🎯 Our Mission

To give every student the chance to play football, cricket, and basketball through training, matches, and a supportive team culture.

💎 Our Values

Inclusivity — open to all students. Team spirit — we win together. Balance — sport alongside studies.
