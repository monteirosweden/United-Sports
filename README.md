# 🏆 Sports United

**Play. Compete. Belong.**

Sports United is the official college club website for **football**, **cricket**, and **basketball**. Whether you are a beginner or a pro, there is a place for you on our team. The site introduces the club, its history, facilities, and gallery, and lets students get in touch.

---

## 📄 Pages

| Page | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **[Home-Page.html](Home-Page.html)** | Welcome landing page with the club's tagline, the three sports we offer, and a "Why Join Us?" section highlighting team spirit, inclusivity, regular practice, and balancing sport with studies. |
| **[About.html](About.html)** | Tells the club's story — founded in 2010 — along with our **Mission** and core **Values** (inclusivity, team spirit, balance). |
| **[facilities.html](facilities.html)** | Showcases all our facilities: Cricket Ground, Football Pitch, Basketball Court, Equipment, Gym, Physio Room, and Clubhouse. |
| **[gallery.html](gallery.html)** | A photo gallery of "Moments at the Club," featuring images from matches and training. |
| **[Contact-Details.html](Contact-Details.html)** | Contact information (location, email, phone, practice times) and a working contact form. |

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎨 **Dark, modern theme** with a consistent blue (`#1e90ff`) accent across all pages.
- 📱 **Responsive layout** that adapts to mobiles and tablets.
- 🎬 **Smooth animations** — fade-in sections and a sliding header on every page.
- 🧭 **Sticky navigation bar** shared across all pages for easy browsing.
- 🖱️ **Interactive hover effects** on cards, buttons, and gallery photos.
- 🏟️ **Sports showcase** — football, cricket, and basketball sections with icons.
- 📷 **Image gallery** with scaling hover effects.
- 📬 **Functional contact form** with a JavaScript success message.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5** — page structure
- **CSS3** — styling, animations, and responsive design (inline styles)
- **JavaScript** — contact form validation and success feedback

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

No build tools or dependencies are required. Simply open any page in your browser:

1. Clone or download the repository.
2. Open `Home-Page.html` in a web browser.
3. Use the navigation bar to explore all pages.
